Questions & answers

Good to know.

Is it an app?

No – on purpose. The Family Planner runs in your phone’s browser. You don’t need to download anything from the App Store or Play Store and you don’t grant any permissions. If you like, you can add it to your home screen with one tap.

Who can see our wall, photos and timetables?

Only phones that have scanned your secret family QR code. Without the code you just see “This wall is private” and can’t write anything. If the code ends up in the wrong hands, create a new one on “My wall” with one tap.

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes. Big buttons, pictures and the read-aloud function make it easy for kids from age 7. A word filter blocks swear words, phone numbers and links. Parents can see every post on “My wall” and delete it.

Does everyone need an account?

No. The family just scans the family code once – no account. Only the person who manages the planner signs in once with Google.

Will it cost anything later?

No. You pay €4.99 once – no subscription, no running costs, no ads.

How many walls do I get?

Every code sets up 2 walls, e.g. one for you and one for the grandparents. Need more? There’s an update: 2 more walls for €4.99.

Can I give the Family Planner as a gift?

Yes. Buy it and forward the email with the code. Whoever redeems the code gets the planner.

Does the emergency button replace a first-aid course?

No. It helps kids remember the most important things in a stressful moment: get an adult, and if in doubt, call the emergency number (112 in Europe, 911 in the USA).

Where is our data stored?

With Google Firebase in a data centre in Frankfurt, Germany. No ads and no selling of data. Photos are made smaller before uploading, and you can delete them anytime. The privacy policy is available in German.
